    Text board & piece positions

    The board spaces are unnumbered. This summary counts track positions clockwise from Alice’s start, from 1–40; protected starts are 1, 11, 21 and 31. Each team then has four distinct safe spots. A piece that reaches one stays safe and cannot move again. All game actions are also available in the turn panel.

    How to play
    1. Enter on 2, 4 or 6. Bring a piece out without a question only if its protected start is empty. Every successful entry earns another roll. Or answer to move a piece already on the board.
    2. Choose, then answer. Lock in a legal piece before its comprehension question. A correct answer moves it forward; a wrong answer moves it backward by the die value, stopping at its start. A blocked backward landing leaves it in place.
    3. Mind your landing. Capture rivals on ordinary stones. Protected starts cannot be shared; your own pieces cannot stack. Land exactly on any free safe spot without overshooting the fourth. Passing an occupied safe spot is allowed.
    4. Four safe wins. Park one piece in each of your four safe spots to win. Safe pieces cannot move or be captured. A correct on-board move on a six also earns another roll; a wrong answer always ends your turn.

    The host chooses chapters and can pause the whole table. Computer players fill empty seats at Go.
